What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help sites remember your actions and preferences over time.

How We Use Cookies

Freelankarx uses cookies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for site functionality (login, forms, security)
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors use our site (anonymized)
  • Preference cookies: To remember your settings and choices

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use (e.g., analytics). These are governed by the respective privacy policies of those services.
