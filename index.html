Common Questions

How long does a project typically take?

Most projects take 3-6 weeks depending on complexity. A simple website takes 2-3 weeks, while a complex Shopify store or custom web app takes 6-12 weeks.

What does it cost to work with Freelankarx?

Projects start at $1,500 for basic websites and range upward based on scope. We provide detailed quotes after the discovery call.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every project is built mobile-first with careful attention to responsive design, touch interactions, and performance.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support packages for all projects. Long-term partnership is part of our philosophy.

Where are you based?

We're based in Nigeria but serve clients globally across 15+ countries.
